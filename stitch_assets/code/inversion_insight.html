The Physics of the Delhi Basin Trap

The Indo-Gangetic Plain forms an enclosed shallow atmospheric trough bordered by the Himalayan uplift to the north and the Aravalli range to the southwest. During winter, calm anticyclonic synoptic winds fail to clear nocturnal katabatic cold-air drainage.

Strong radiative heat loss from the soil cools the surface faster than overlying air layers. This creates an impenetrable thermal subsidence inversion lid that compresses vehicular, industrial, and agrarian stubble plumes within a shallow 300-meter micro-trench.
